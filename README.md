## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Backend Engineer with over 8 years of experience designing, building, and operating high-throughput, low-latency distributed systems. I specialize in creating scalable, secure, and cost-effective backend architectures for cybersecurity and enterprise platforms.

- 🏗 **Architecture:** Microservices, Event-Driven Architecture, CQRS, Outbox Pattern
- ⚙️ **Focus:** High Concurrency, Low-Latency Optimization, and Cloud Cost Reduction
- 🔒 **Security:** OAuth2, JWT, Secure API Design, IAM Integrations
- 📍 **Location:** Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam 🇻🇳

## 🧠 Core Expertise & Technical Knowledge

- **System Design & Architecture:** Extensive experience applying Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Hexagonal Architecture, and scaling microservices from the ground up. I specialize in robust design patterns that decouple services and handle complex business logic cleanly.
- **Event-Driven & Messaging:** Deep knowledge in building resilient asynchronous workflows using custom RabbitMQ/SQS implementations. Adept at applying CQRS and Outbox patterns for reliable data synchronization, idempotency, and high-throughput event processing.
- **Performance Optimization:** Proven track record of identifying and optimizing system bottlenecks. I have led migrations of core services from Java to Go, drastically reducing cloud infrastructure costs (up to 3x) while strictly adhering to p95 latencies under 200ms.
- **Security & Identity Deployment:** Experienced in building centralized authentication/authorization systems, RBAC, and integrating enterprise SSO solutions (Okta, MS Entra ID). Well-versed in deploying applications across both public clouds and strict, air-gapped on-premise environments.
